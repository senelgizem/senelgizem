### About

I am a remote sensing scientist with a PhD in Geomatics Engineering, currently based in Spain. My work combines Earth Observation data with machine learning to produce reliable, interpretable information for agricultural policy, environmental monitoring, and post-crisis assessment.

My focus is on multi-sensor time series (Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 multispectral), from large-scale data extraction in Google Earth Engine to model development, statistical validation, and reproducible research code. 

### Research areas

- **Agricultural monitoring** - crop-type mapping, declaration verification, early-season classification, drought robustness
- **Deep learning for Earth Observation** - recurrent and mixture-of-experts models for satellite time series, segmentation, object-based image analysis
- **Crisis and impact assessment** - geospatial analytics for mine action, soil impact, and agricultural production loss

### Selected projects

**[rs-moe-crop-declaration](https://github.com/senelgizem/rs-moe-crop-declaration)**
Interpretable Mixture-of-Experts models that flag mismatches between declared and observed crops using Sentinel-1/2 time series across France and Spain. Includes Earth Engine extraction, PyTorch Lightning training, and scripts reproducing every result in the accompanying paper.
`PyTorch Lightning` `Google Earth Engine` `Sentinel-1/2` `Time series`

**[sentinel-ml-greenhouse](https://github.com/senelgizem/sentinel-ml-greenhouse)**
Benchmark of Random Forest, LightGBM, and XGBoost on Sentinel-derived spectral, SAR, and temporal features for greenhouse detection, based on my doctoral research.
`scikit-learn` `LightGBM` `XGBoost` `Feature engineering`
